Install AgentsInTheCloud as an app

Open AgentsInTheCloud in its own window and launch it from your home screen or apps.

  1. Tap … → Share (or Share directly in the Top/Bottom tab layout).
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen. Turn on Open as Web App, then tap Add.
  3. Missing the option? Find it under Edit Actions in the Share menu.
